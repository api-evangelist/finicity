# Finicity (Mastercard Open Banking, US)

> Finicity, LLC is a Utah-based financial-data aggregator and open-finance platform, acquired by Mastercard in 2020 and now operating as Mastercard Open Banking (US). It provides consumer-permissioned account and transaction aggregation, Verification of Assets/Income/Employment, cash-flow analysis, bank statements, and account validation for ACH payments over the api.finicity.com host. Auth is a two-header API-key model (Finicity-App-Key + a short-lived Finicity-App-Token). A founding FDX participant; FCRA-regulated CRA for its verification reports.

## APIs
- [Aggregation (Accounts & Transactions)](https://developer.mastercard.com/open-banking-us/documentation/): consumer-permissioned account/transaction aggregation.
- [Finicity Connect](https://developer.mastercard.com/open-banking-us/documentation/connect/): hosted, embeddable account-linking + consent experience.
- [Verification of Assets (VoA)](https://developer.mastercard.com/open-banking-us/documentation/): permissioned asset-verification reports for lending.
- [Verification of Income & Employment (VoIE)](https://developer.mastercard.com/open-banking-us/documentation/): income/employment signals from transaction + payroll data.
- [Transaction Analysis (Cash Flow)](https://developer.mastercard.com/open-banking-us/documentation/): cash-flow, income and expense decisioning insights.
- [Payments (Account Validation)](https://developer.mastercard.com/open-banking-us/documentation/): account ownership/status validation for ACH.
- [Open Banking Data Access (FDX)](https://developer.mastercard.com/open-banking-us/documentation/): FDX-aligned data sharing in support of CFPB 1033.
